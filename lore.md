# Game Lore

## The Gist of the Story

You are a diamond hunter, hoping to loot the CreepQueen's lair for treasures and other valuables.

You follow in the footsteps of another party of adventurers who also dared to enter the crypt. Scattered throughout the depths lie forgotten scrolls, remnants of those who came before. Their faded ink whispers of astonishing discoveries, imminent dangers, and tragic ends that still echo through the stone corridors.

Stories and tall tales of what became of them:

- **Sonya** died on level 5, against Mr Demon
- **Marco** was crushed on level 8
- The **narrator** and a few more members of the party continued on
- The narrator **never reached the CreepQueen**

The CreepQueen — wasn't she supposed to be dead? She's not. And you have just walked into a trap.

## The Party

- **The Narrator** — a member of the first party and its sole chronicler. The scrolls are his journal, and he is the one who "continued on" — never reaching the CreepQueen.
- **Sonya** — the barrel expert. Her notes survive even where her soul does not.
- **Marco** — the pathfinder, the one who found the hidden passages.
- **Jon** — the one who saw the Demon's weakness.
- **You** — the diamond hunter now in the crypt, reading their words by torchlight.

## The Descent

The first scroll lies near the entrance, in the safe place where the magical purple stones keep the monsters out. The warning is plain: get all the diamonds before time runs out. The ink is kind but firm — swimming across the water is not healthy, but sometimes necessary. It is a merciful start, and it does not last.

Past the entrance, a ghost bars the way, and the party learns the only answer is fire. Hit the barrels to blow them up and escape — Sonya's note, calm as ever, records the blast radius at two meters. In the margin, someone has scratched: *Diamond Hunter. Grab all the Diamonds!!!!*

The third lesson is practical: the green key on the right opens the green door on the left, and hidden treasures are out there if you know where to look. *Grab all the diamonds to complete this map. Avoid the monsters and other hazards.* *Other* hazards — there is more than meets the eye.

Then comes the Strange Garden, and the ink goes dark.

*Eerie dread entering the garden. Death struck our companions when they least expected it.* The CreepQueen's crafty followers have laced the ground with traps. Red eyes gaze at you, everywhere — *Be careful, Jon* — and the monsters here are wrong. They don't move like the ones the party met before. *What madness is this?* Somewhere among the plants, a voice that is not quite human offers help: *One final push is needed. Fear not... completing your task is at hand... friend.* *Death by Proximity Awaits. Bountiful Treasures Lead Us Astray.... Forgetting Reasons.* Marco, the pathfinder, marks the chute into a secret room whose floor is heavy with treasures. Reap what you sow.

In Fire & Ice, Jon finds out that the Demon is vulnerable to water and muses over the irony — ice to fight fire. The boulders can be moved, albeit very slowly, and the party wonders if they could be turned to their use. But there is no way out of that chamber. They are going to have to fight this thing. And it is there that Sonya is cornered, and falls. The last entry in Marco's hand:

*"Maybe The Divine Bless Her Soul. -- Marco"*

The Swamp is worse. Flora that absorbs monsters and infests the water. *I never imagined such horrors.* The notes warn that fear of being trapped is not healthy, and that sometimes you must use your own body to forge a path. But the party presses on: *Almost there now. I knew we could make it to safety. These evil plants are truly the Devil's.*

The pace quickens. Fruits give a sugar rush — energy up, faster feet for a little while — and the monsters, predictable creatures on their endless rounds, can be slipped past through their blind spots. Buried in the dirt, they find a resurrection mushroom: *I never imagined... you can spawn an extra life. Very handy in these parts.*

Then, at the Pressure Points, Marco is struck. He dies in an instant. *Our numbers grow smaller.* *In Memory of Marco. You'll Be Missed.*

Beyond the Deathtrap, where sometimes water cannot be avoided, a rumour abounds of a secret passage hiding a relic. The party follows it. The scrolls do not say what they found.

In the Smart Puzzles, a riddle is scrawled in haste: *Getting Wet is the way to become Dry.* Approach the maze with care — its denizens may surprise. *I fear what has become of us... These Cyclops are quick... You Must Out Maneuver Them.* In the next chamber, the rigid plants might, if used to advantage, turn against the dungeon itself — and there is something too tempting to ignore. *Not a chance... maybe it could be a blessing in disguise.*

At the Perilous Choices, no scroll survives. Whatever choice was made there, it was made in silence.

By the Mad Dash, the ink is frantic. Open the passages, take the diamonds — *Time is short... growing shorter.* *Be careful... they want you to take the bait... you should know better by now. We lost so much already.* At the Crossroads, a single line: *Strange sense of danger entering this place...*

And then — nothing. The notes for the Confused end. The notes for the doom end. The party's last words were written nowhere, save one final warning, scrawled across the last page of the Scavenger Hunt:

*"Heavens will open and swallow you!"*

That was the last thing the first party left behind.

## Deeper Still

The scrolls that follow are in a different hand. The party's story is over, and the hunter — the one who goes deeper — is back in the depths, terse and practical: use the green key to open the green door, exploit the monsters' blind spots. *Find all the diamonds to leave this map and move on to the next area in your journey.* Picking up items may reveal secrets, hidden items, more collectibles — and perhaps even more. The flowers are a mercy: health bonuses, some small relief from the damage of minions.

In the lowest layers, the hunter writes down what he has seen: dragons coming out of eggs, breathing fire, moving very fast, wielding lots of damage. *I never expected these new monsters to be this powerful.*

After vanquishing the CreepQueen, our relentless diamond hunter delves deeper into the ancient dungeon — where untold riches shimmer in the shadows, new adventures beckon, and lurking perils grow ever more cunning.

The dragons have only just begun to hatch.
